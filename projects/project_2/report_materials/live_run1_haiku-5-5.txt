new numbness, weakness, or trouble controlling your bladder or bowels?"
A Patient With Chronic Back Pain: Thank you for fixing the timeline. That's the

back, locked up tight, but my left leg has been going numb down to the outside

times a week, and it comes and goes.

No weakness that I've noticed, though I did drop my coffee cup twice last month
and blamed the handle. No trouble with my bladder or bowels. I'd tell you if 
there were, because that would be the first thing I'd want looked at, not 
something I'd sit on.
